# Audio Capture, Denoising, and Silero VAD Rules## 1. Forbidden Audio Patterns- **NO `MediaRecorder`:** Do not encode slices as `audio/webm` or `audio/mp4`; container headers fail downstream decoding in streaming chunks.- **NO Main-Thread Processing:** Do not use `ScriptProcessorNode` on the main UI thread; use `AudioWorklet` on a dedicated audio thread to avoid UI jank and audio drops.- **NO 1-Second Input Buffering:** Never wait 1 second before emitting audio. Emit **20–40 ms** frames continuously.## 2. AudioWorklet Capture & Resampling- Request microphone access via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with mono capture (`channelCount: 1`) and selectable hardware constraints (`echoCancellation`, `autoGainControl`, `noiseSuppression`).- Reuse a single `AudioContext` instance across sessions to eliminate startup delay.- Inside the `AudioWorkletProcessor`:  - Downsample browser-native `Float32Array` frames cleanly to **16,000 Hz**.  - Support **Whisper Mode (Quiet Speech):** when toggled on, apply a configurable software gain boost with soft clipping protection before VAD so subvocalized/quiet speech is captured cleanly.  - Convert normalized `Float32` (`-1.0` to `1.0`) samples into signed 16-bit little-endian PCM (`Int16Array`).## 3. Noise Suppression & Silero VAD Pipeline1. **RNNoise (WASM):** Filter incoming audio frames through RNNoise WASM to suppress room fans, background conversations, street noise, and keyboard clicks before speech reaches VAD or ASR.2. **Silero VAD:** Run Silero VAD on the denoised audio stream:   - Keep a short pre-speech ring buffer so the first syllable is not clipped when speech onset triggers.   - Expose configurable VAD sensitivity thresholds (standard vs. Whisper Mode) and tune end-of-turn hangover near ~120 ms for responsive turn detection.   - **Clause Slicing for Long Dictations:** During multi-minute dictations, slice cleanly at natural speech pauses and dispatch completed clauses asynchronously to ASR so transcription finishes progressively while the speaker continues.   - **Zero-Silence Guarantee:** Never dispatch audio buffers containing zero VAD-detected speech to Whisper-family models (prevents silence hallucinations).
